## Rules this direction inherits

- The palette is one accent plus neutrals derived from the theme. Every image on
  the page is graded toward that palette so the set reads as one shoot.
- Two type families maximum, and the scale jump between display and body is
  obvious at a glance.
- Spacing is an 8px system. More space above a heading than below it.
- Exactly the two motion behaviours above. A third is scope creep.
- No section repeats the anchor directly above it.
- Every image is a real photograph or a real rendered asset. No gray boxes, no
  `bg-gradient` standing in for a picture, no emoji as an icon system.
